The name

The name never came from the galaxy

An orrery is a clockwork model of the heavens: turn the crank and every body lands in an exact, reproducible position.

That is concept.md, 23 September, the day orrery was named. The machine above is that sentence: the template is the clockwork, every pick is a planet on its own gear, the seed is the crank. The galaxy only borrowed the sky. Without it the name still fits, because it was always about the seed.

Recommendation: keep orrery, and never let it stand alone. Always “orrery, a screenplay with dice in it”. Write the pronunciation once in the README (OR-uh-ree), and give the CLI another PyPI name when it ships. The film-and-dice names are crowded: Roll is an AI video app, Rollfilm an open-source AI photo manager, Zoetrope is Coppola’s studio. No ComfyUI or AI-video project is called orrery.

The galaxy becomes the gallery (#89), as decided. If the film words win, dailies is cinema’s word for the same thing: the day’s takes, watched and judged.

Positioning · April Dunford’s canvas

What orrery is, seen from outside

Without it, people use
prompts written one by one · wildcard nodes that roll blind and forget · a chatbot asked for ten prompts · clips chained by hand in a node graph · hosted storyboard tools that rewrite your prompt
Only orrery
one text file rolls a different result for every seed, and the same seed always rolls the same one · every choice keeps its name, so one can change while the rest stays · the script is a screenplay (SCENE, CUT TO, END ON, REMEMBER) compiled to the model’s own format · a reel branches, loops and rolls its own path, forever · every output carries its script
Worth to them
Never run out (one idea, endless takes) · never lose one (replay any take, remix it one choice at a time) · stories, not clips (a film that holds together and keeps going)
Who cares most
People making AI video in ComfyUI on their own GPU who post series: a show, a format, a character. Next to them, generative artists who already think in seeds.
Category
Not “wildcards”, which says random and small. A subcategory people already know: a screenplay with dice in it. By analogy, seeds for films: a Minecraft seed is a world from a number, an orrery seed is a film from a script.

The README today leads with “learns your taste”, the one attribute others could copy. A reproducible, branching screenplay is the part nobody else has.

The words

Lines to choose from

Pyro’s “One prompt, infinite content” has the right shape. Two words work against it: prompt sounds like one string, and content is the autoposters’ word (“one script becomes infinite content” sells faceless YouTube channels). 2026 is the year of the slop backlash: “Your AI Slop Bores Me” drew 50M hits, and Fruit Love Island was called slop and taken down.

Reach · scored with Jonah Berger’s STEPPS

Six ways to reach people

Each dot is one driver of sharing: social currency, triggers, emotion, public, practical value, stories. Full dot strong, half dot present. What spread in the precedents: a short, spoiler-free result (a seed, a code, a grid), the same thing for everyone, proof you found it first (Infinite Craft’s “First Discovery!”), a live thing to drop into, and one clip on Reddit to set it off.
